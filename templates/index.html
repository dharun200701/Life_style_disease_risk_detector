How does the prediction work?

Your information is analyzed by a trained Random Forest machine learning model. SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) is then used to identify which features contributed most to the prediction.

Random Forest Classifier

The proposed system uses Random Forest as the final prediction model because of its strong classification performance and ability to handle multiple lifestyle and health features.

Explainable AI with SHAP

SHAP explains individual predictions by showing how each input feature increases or decreases the predicted sleep disorder risk.

Privacy Focused

User information is processed during the prediction process and is not permanently stored by the system.

This system is developed for educational and health-awareness purposes. Predictions should not be considered a medical diagnosis or a replacement for professional medical advice.
